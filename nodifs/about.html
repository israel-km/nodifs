About NODIFS

A bilingual school community shaped by curiosity, respect and a global outlook.

Our story

A world of possibility, rooted in Kampala.

Nova Dhech International French School (NODIFS) brings French and English learning together in a supportive international environment. We believe every learner deserves to be known, challenged and encouraged to find their strengths.

Our school community values academic rigour, cultural exchange and the confidence to ask meaningful questions. We work with families to help students grow into thoughtful, capable and compassionate citizens.
