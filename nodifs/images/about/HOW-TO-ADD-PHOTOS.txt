ABOUT PAGE BACKGROUND PHOTOS
============================
The About page rotates 5 background photos. Right now it shows 5 illustrated
placeholders (about-1.svg ... about-5.svg).

To use your real photos, save them in THIS folder with these exact names:

   about-1.jpg   about-2.jpg   about-3.jpg   about-4.jpg   about-5.jpg

The site uses a .jpg automatically when it exists (the .svg is only a fallback).
No code changes needed.

Tips
- Landscape photos, about 1920 x 1080 px, each under ~400 KB (compress at squoosh.app).
- Keep the main subject on the RIGHT half; the headline sits on the left.
- Change the caption under each photo in about.html (data-caption="...").
- Slide speed: --slide-time in css/about-motion.css (default 6500ms).
