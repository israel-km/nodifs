TEAM PHOTOS
===========
Save each person's photo here, using the file name written in data/team.json
(for example images/team/amina-mbuyi.jpg).

- Square or portrait photos work best (about 800 x 800 px, under ~200 KB).
- Keep the face in the upper half of the picture.
- If a photo is missing, the card shows the person's initials instead.

To add a person: copy one block in data/team.json, change the name, photo, role and bio.
To translate: add "sw", "ln" or "lg" next to "en" and "fr" in role/bio. Languages with no
text yet automatically show the English version.
